Your first class

Good questions

Do I need any experience?

No. Every program starts from the basics, and drills are scaled so beginners and experienced students can train in the same session.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes you can move in, water and a small towel. For bo staff sessions, bring a staff if you have one; if not, ask in your DM.

Is self-defence training safe for beginners?

Yes. Techniques are taught slowly, with control and a willing partner, before any speed is added. Nobody is forced into drills they aren't comfortable with.

Can you train our college, office or team?

Yes. Kunal runs on-site workshops for institutions and teams, including a session for the Indore Police Force. DM “WORKSHOP” with your group size and dates.

How do I join?

Send a DM on Instagram with the word for your program: TRAIN, SAFE, WORKSHOP or PRIVATE. Kunal will reply with timings, location and fees.
